# ShopEasy Shipping Policy

ShopEasy usually ships orders within 1-2 business days after payment is confirmed.
Standard delivery typically takes 3-7 business days, depending on the destination.

Orders over $50 qualify for free standard shipping. Orders of $50 or less have a
standard shipping fee of $5. Express shipping is available at checkout for an
additional $12 and usually arrives within 1-2 business days after dispatch.

Customers can find their tracking link in the shipping confirmation email. If an
order has not arrived within 10 business days of dispatch, contact ShopEasy Support
with the order number so the team can investigate.
